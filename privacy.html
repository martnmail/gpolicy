Privacy Policy for rclone-gdrive

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Scope of Access

This app uses the Google Drive API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This limits the app to only accessing files and folders that it itself creates or uploads. It cannot view, modify, or delete any other files in the user's Google Drive.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to third parties. Files accessed through the Drive API remain solely within the user's own Google Drive account.

Data Sharing

No data is shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party.

Data Retention

Files remain in the user's Google Drive until manually deleted by the account owner.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner via the linked GitHub repository.
